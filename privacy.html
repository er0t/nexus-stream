Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026 • Applies to Nexus HD & Nexus TV

1. Overview & Commitment

Nexus HD (Mobile & Tablet) and Nexus TV (Android TV & Fire TV) are committed to respecting and protecting user privacy. Our applications are designed with zero mandatory registration, no account creation, and no collection of personally identifiable information (PII) such as real names, home addresses, phone numbers, or credit card information.

2. Local Device Storage

All watch status records (such as completed episodes, watch history, and resume playback timestamps) are saved purely in your device's local memory (SharedPreferences). This data never leaves your device and is not synchronized to external personal user databases.

3. Advertising & Analytics (Start.io)

Nexus is ad-supported and free to use without subscription fees. We integrate industry-standard ad SDKs (such as Start.io) to deliver banner and interstitial advertisements. These SDKs may collect non-personal, anonymized technical telemetry such as device model, operating system version, coarse geographic location (country or region), and advertising identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID) solely for ad delivery, fraud prevention, and frequency capping.

For more details on Start.io's privacy practices, please visit the Start.io Privacy Policy. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized interest-based advertising at any time via your Android or Fire TV Device Settings.

4. Stream Indexing & Third-Party Content Disclaimer

Nexus HD and Nexus TV operate exclusively as a client-side media scraper and player interface. We do not host, store, upload, or manage any audiovisual media files on our own servers. All stream links and metadata are indexed from publicly accessible online third-party repositories. We do not control or endorse the content hosted by these external services.

5. Contact & Inquiries

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or crawler verification, please review our official publisher file at /app-ads.txt or contact our technical team at support@nexus-stream.internal.
